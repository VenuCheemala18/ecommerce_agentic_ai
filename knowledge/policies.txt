E-COMMERCE RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

Customers can request a return within 7 days of delivery.

Damaged products are eligible for return or replacement.

Customers may need to provide photos of damaged products.

Refunds are processed after the returned product is verified.

The refund amount normally equals the amount paid for the product.

Orders that have already been shipped may not always be cancelled.

Delivered orders cannot normally be cancelled.

Refund processing can take several business days depending on the payment method.

Replacement is available for eligible damaged or defective products.

If an issue cannot be resolved automatically, it should be escalated to a human support executive.
